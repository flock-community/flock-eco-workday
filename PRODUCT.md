# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Flock Software Engineering employees and consultants, plus admins/HR/finance. They use Workday on the desktop during the workweek to log hours, leave, sick days and expenses, manage clients/projects/assignments/contracts, and read aggregated reports and dashboards. Most are technically literate; they want to get in, do a recurring data-entry or review task quickly, and get out. Admin roles do heavier review and oversight work across many people.

## Product Purpose

Flock Workday is a standalone workforce-management application: time tracking, leave and sickness, expenses, contracts/assignments, and reporting. Success is low-friction recurring data entry, trustworthy aggregated numbers, and review screens that admins can scan fast. It is an internal tool that should still feel cared-for and on-brand, not a generic admin template.

## Brand Personality

Flock. Confident, warm, a little playful. Three words: bold, friendly, precise. The signature is the bright Flock yellow (#fcde00) used with conviction, giving an otherwise utilitarian tool a human, crafted touch. Voice is direct and lightly irreverent (the existing session-expiry copy calls the user a "peasant"), never corporate-bland.

## Anti-references

- Default Material Design templates: stock MUI look, uppercase buttons, harsh flat shadows, pure #000/#fff, blue-grey everything.
- Generic enterprise admin dashboards (SAP/Jira-grey density with no identity).
- Over-decorated SaaS marketing aesthetics: gradient-text hero metrics, glassmorphism, identical card grids.

## Design Principles

- **Yellow with intent.** The Flock yellow is the brand and stays prominent, but it signals (brand surfaces, primary action, highlight) rather than smothering. Every yellow placement is a deliberate choice, not a fill-by-default.
- **Calm density.** Data-entry and review screens reward fast scanning: clear hierarchy, generous-but-consistent rhythm, quiet neutrals so the yellow and the data stand out.
- **Crafted, not corporate.** Keep the human touches (the cheeky copy). The tool should feel made by people who care.
- **One coherent system.** Colour, type, spacing, elevation and components come from shared tokens so every screen inherits the polish, including future ones.
- **Light and dark, both first-class.** Neutrals are tinted toward the brand hue; the yellow reads well on warm off-white and warm near-black alike.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. The brand yellow is light, so it is never used as a background behind white text or as low-contrast text on white; dark text on yellow and yellow accents on dark surfaces carry it instead. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`. Maintain visible focus states and AA contrast for body text and controls in both themes.
